### Registration packages

| Path | Brand (one-time) | Campaign vetting (per submission) | Campaign monthly |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Standard** | **$46** (includes secondary vetting) | **$15** / submission | **$1.50–$10** |

**Campaign vetting is per submission.** The **$15** fee is charged on each campaign submission and each resubmission. It is not a single lifetime fee for that campaign.

**Operator note (practical guidance, not a Twilio SLA):** Common operator experience is roughly **~4 campaign submissions** before a permanent deny rather than a remediable reject. After about three burns (~$45), the client may have **one attempt left** before that brand path is dead and they need a new legal entity. SKILL §6 (“do not loop the same submission”) is arithmetic, not just hygiene.

## Vertical risk

- None of Bandwidth, Twilio, or Telnyx publishes a **cheaper dating tier**.
- Bandwidth: dating-style traffic usually **Standard or Special at $20/mo**, with MNO scrutiny on special use cases.
- Twilio: dating / adult-adjacent content is often treated as **prohibited** under CTIA SHAFT (sex, hate, alcohol, firearms, tobacco). Rejections may be **non-remediable** — not a fee discount.
- Telnyx: the standard and social use-case rows on the TCR table are **$10/mo**, the same as other standard campaigns. A content rejection is a triage outcome (`templates/rejection-triage.md`), not a discount. T-Mobile content-violation fines are listed on the Telnyx fees article.
- Dating and social dating stay the highest-scrutiny test case in the skill. They are not a separate product. Ordinary alerts use the same fee sheet and the same pass-through rule.
- Always separate **registration fees** from **carrier per-segment fees** (AT&T / T-Mobile / Verizon), which are additional.

## Pricing pack (Agent SMS Compliance)

Starter, Pro, and Enterprise are **our** monthly SaaS tiers. They pay for process work. They do not include TCR, CSP, vetting, campaign-review, monthly campaign, or carrier fees, and they must not be quoted as if they did.

| SaaS tier | Monthly | Process included | Pass-through (client pays at CSP cost, on top) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Starter** | **$49** | Intake, preflight, one brand and one campaign filing assist, one remediable rejection pass, case-tracker row | Brand, vetting, each campaign submission or downstream review, monthly campaign fees, initial multi-month minimums, carrier surcharges |
| **Pro** | **$199** | Starter, plus more than one campaign and repeat preflight on remediable resubmits | Same |
| **Enterprise** | **$499** | Pro, plus per-client isolation (pool, keys or Managed Account, STOP list, cooling period) and priority human escalation | Same, plus any CSP plan minimum required for that isolation feature (for example Telnyx Managed Accounts on a Telnyx commit plan) |

Pass-through language for quotes and invoices:

- Name the CSP and the **As-of** date from this file.
- List each third-party line (brand, campaign review, monthly campaign, and the initial three months where that CSP bills them).
- Say that a resubmit can repeat the campaign-review fee: Twilio on each submission, Telnyx on each downstream carrier review.
- Say that a rejection does not convert pass-through into a SaaS credit. The contract warranty is process only (`contracts/draft-msa-clauses.md`).
- Do not fold these amounts into $49, $199, or $499.

Illustrative pass-through, not a bundle price: a Telnyx low-volume mixed campaign quoted from the 2026-09-24 fetch is a **$4.50** brand application, **$15** for each downstream review, and **$1.50/mo** with three months billed initially. A Twilio standard brand path quoted the same day is **$46** plus **$15** per submission plus the monthly use-case fee (**$10** for standard). Re-fetch the articles before sending the quote.
