# 10DLC registration pricing comparison

**As of:** mid-to-late September 2026 (Bandwidth fees page `dateModified` 2026-09-16).  
**Currency:** USD.  
**Scope:** Brand + campaign registration fees (not per-segment carrier surcharges).  
**Sources:** Bandwidth Help Center “10DLC Fees”; Twilio A2P 10DLC product page.

Rates change. Re-check CSP articles before quoting a client.

## Dating / social vertical notes

- Neither Bandwidth nor Twilio publishes a **cheaper dating tier**.
- Bandwidth: dating-style traffic usually **Standard or Special at $20/mo**, with MNO scrutiny on special use cases.
- Twilio: dating / adult-adjacent content is often treated as **prohibited** under CTIA SHAFT (e.g. error families around sex/adult content, content violation). Rejections may be **non-remediable**—not a fee discount.
- Always separate **registration fees** from **carrier per-segment fees** (AT&T / T-Mobile / Verizon), which are additional.
