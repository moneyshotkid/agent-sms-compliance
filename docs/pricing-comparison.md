# 10DLC registration pricing comparison

**As of:** 2026-09-24 (America/Los_Angeles).  
**Currency:** USD.  
**Scope:** Brand + campaign registration fees (not per-segment carrier surcharges).  

**Verified this refresh:**

- Bandwidth Help Center “10DLC Fees” — https://www.bandwidth.com/support/en/articles/12823086-10dlc-fees — article `dateModified` 2026-09-16. Registration amounts below match that article. No Bandwidth dollar amounts changed.
- Twilio Help “Pricing and Fees for A2P 10DLC Service” — https://support.twilio.com/hc/en-us/articles/1260803965530-Pricing-and-Fees-for-A2P-10DLC-Service — article updated 2026-06-15. **Quote this article** for Twilio registration fees.
- Twilio brand docs — https://www.twilio.com/docs/trust-hub/registrations/a2p-10dlc-brand — TCR brand registration **$4.50** and Standard brand vetting **$41.50**, which add up to the Help Center Standard brand fee of **$46**.
- Twilio product page — https://www.twilio.com/en-us/phone-numbers/a2p-10dlc — re-checked 2026-09-24. It still prints the pre-2025-08-01 bundles (Standard brand $44, Low-volume and Sole Proprietor brand $4). Those figures disagree with the Help Center article. Do not quote them.

Rates change. Re-check the cited CSP articles before quoting a client. Do not invent carrier, TCR, or CSP fees.

## Dating / social vertical notes

- Neither Bandwidth nor Twilio publishes a **cheaper dating tier**.
- Bandwidth: dating-style traffic usually **Standard or Special at $20/mo**, with MNO scrutiny on special use cases.
- Twilio: dating / adult-adjacent content is often treated as **prohibited** under CTIA SHAFT (sex, hate, alcohol, firearms, tobacco). Rejections may be **non-remediable** — not a fee discount.
- Always separate **registration fees** from **carrier per-segment fees** (AT&T / T-Mobile / Verizon), which are additional.
