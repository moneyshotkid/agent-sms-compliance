# Campaign description boilerplate — dating / social verticals

**Risk:** High. Many CSPs (notably Twilio A2P) treat adult, escort, and some dating content as **prohibited** under CTIA SHAFT. Use only if the product is legitimate dating/social coaching or notifications with real opt-in—not unsolicited outreach.

## Campaign description (edit brackets)

```
[Brand] sends SMS to users who create an account on [website] and explicitly opt in to SMS
for [dating coach replies / match notifications / conversation reminders]. Messages are
one-to-one or user-triggered coaching related to the user’s own conversations. We do not
send blast marketing to purchased lists. Users opt in via [checkbox on signup / settings
toggle] with disclosure of frequency and STOP instructions. HELP provides support contact;
STOP opts the user out immediately.
```

## Sample messages

1. `[Brand]: Hi {{first_name}}, your wingman draft is ready. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.`
2. `[Brand]: Reminder — you asked for a follow-up tip for your chat with {{contact}}. Open the app to review. Msg frequency varies. STOP to cancel.`
3. `[Brand]: You opted in to SMS coaching. We’ll only text about your active threads. Reply STOP anytime.`

## What not to put in samples

- Sexual / explicit language
- Guaranteed dates, “meet singles tonight” spam patterns
- Affiliate lead-gen to third parties without separate consent
- Scraped phone numbers or “we found your profile” cold outreach

## Use-case picker notes

- Prefer **Low-Volume Mixed** only if volume truly is low and messages mix transactional + reminders.
- Prefer a single declared use case that matches the product.
- If CSP offers **Proxy** / conversational special use cases, confirm eligibility with the CSP before filing.
